WHAT THIS PAGE DOES:
- Dedicated Community Notifications page, separate from normal Notifications.
- Chronological notifications from all joined Communities.
- Community name/icon are deliberately prominent on every notification.
- Quick Like and Quick Reply work on reply / mention items.
- Announcements stay simple and open the Community.
- Moderator/system activity can appear without becoming a dashboard.
- Deleted Community notifications remain visible in history.
- Deleted Community notifications have NO reply box and NO dead Community link.
- Reads deletion notifications stored under allMediaCommunityNotificationsV1 from the Create/Edit Community flow.
- Mark all as read works and persists locally.
- Pinned Community bells open a filtered quick-notification window.
- The quick window links to this page filtered to that one Community.
- Community chips still open the Community itself.
- More still contains joined Communities and Pin/Unpin.

PROTOTYPE NOTE:
The page includes sample notification data so the design can be reviewed immediately.
Stored Community deletion events from the prototype are inserted above the samples automatically.
