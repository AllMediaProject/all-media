✦Why I Keep a Tiny Autumn Sketchbook

AUTUMN SKETCHBOOK

I started carrying a tiny sketchbook this fall because I wanted somewhere to put all the little ideas that never felt big enough for a full project…

There is something about a tiny sketchbook that makes drawing feel less serious. A page does not have to become a finished piece, and an idea does not have to prove that it deserves a whole canvas.

Lately mine has become a tiny archive of crooked houses, sleepy moons, leaves, pumpkins and color notes.

About Spooky Cozy

A year-round place for people who love Halloween, cozy spooky things, seasonal art, crafts, collecting, decorating, and everything a little haunted. Share what you're making, find inspiration, ask questions, and hang out with people who never quite put Halloween away.

What belongs here

Decorations and seasonal setups, crafts and DIY projects, collections and vintage finds, art and creative work, recipes and cozy treats, questions, discussion and spooky chit-chat.
