About Spooky Cozy

A year-round place for people who love Halloween, cozy spooky things, seasonal art, crafts, collecting, decorating, and everything a little haunted. Share what you're making, find inspiration, ask questions, and hang out with people who never quite put Halloween away.

What belongs here

Decorations and seasonal setups, crafts and DIY projects, collections and vintage finds, art and photography, cozy recipes, questions, discussions, and spooky little everyday moments.

Community Rules

1 · Keep it spooky & cozy

Keep posts relevant to Halloween, spooky themes, cozy horror, seasonal crafts, collecting, or closely related interests.

2 · Credit the creator

When sharing work you didn't make, clearly credit the original artist or source.

3 · Be kind to beginners

Constructive advice is welcome. Mocking someone's experience or skill level isn't.

4 · Keep criticism constructive

Critique the work or idea, not the person.
