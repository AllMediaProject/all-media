✦Why I Keep a Tiny Autumn Sketchbook

HOME PREVIEW IMAGE

I started carrying a tiny sketchbook this fall because I wanted somewhere to put all the little ideas that never felt big enough for a full project. It has slowly become one of my favorite creative habits…

There is something about a tiny sketchbook that makes drawing feel less serious. A page does not have to become a finished piece, and an idea does not have to prove that it deserves a whole canvas.

Lately I have been filling mine with crooked little houses, sleepy moons, leaves, color notes, and scraps of ideas I might come back to later. Some probably will never become anything else—and I think that is part of why I like it.

It has become a small place for creativity without pressure: something I can open, add to, and close again whenever an idea wanders by.
