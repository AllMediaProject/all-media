✦Why I Keep a Tiny Autumn Sketchbook

AUTUMN SKETCHBOOK

I started carrying a tiny sketchbook this fall because I wanted somewhere to put all the little ideas that never felt big enough for a full project…

There is something about a tiny sketchbook that makes drawing feel less serious. A page does not have to become a finished piece, and an idea does not have to prove that it deserves a whole canvas.

Lately mine has become a tiny archive of crooked houses, sleepy moons, leaves, pumpkins and color notes.
